A LITTLE ABOUT ME

From Engineering to Executive Support

My journey into executive support didn’t begin in a virtual office.

I hold a degree in Computer & Communication Engineering, and I started my career in high-precision manufacturing, where accuracy, efficiency, and attention to detail were essential. Working in leadership and team administration taught me how to coordinate people and processes, manage demanding schedules, maintain quality standards, and keep operations moving under pressure.

But along the way, I discovered that what I enjoyed most was bringing structure to complex work and helping things run more smoothly.

That realization led me from manufacturing into digital operations and virtual assistance—a transition that allowed me to combine my technical background with my strengths in organization, coordination, communication, and problem-solving.

Today, I support business owners with the operational details that keep their businesses moving: coordinating projects, tracking priorities and deadlines, preparing reports, managing digital content, maintaining Kajabi websites, organizing information, and making sure important follow-ups don't get lost.

Why Executive Support?

Because behind every growing business are countless details competing for attention.

I understand that your time is valuable. My role is to help bring structure, consistency, and follow-through to the moving parts behind your business so you can spend more time focusing on your clients, your growth, and the work that matters most to you.

I bring an engineering mindset to virtual assistance: I look for ways to organize information, improve workflows, anticipate what needs attention, and create systems that make everyday operations easier to manage.

For me, being a reliable executive partner isn't about simply checking tasks off a list.

It's about paying attention, staying one step ahead, and becoming someone you can trust with the details.
